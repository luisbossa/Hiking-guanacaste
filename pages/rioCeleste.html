Why Choose This Tour?

This is one of the most iconic and beautiful day trips in Costa Rica. You'll experience the magic of Río Celeste from every angle and witness natural wonders like the Teñideros and the ancient Tree of Peace.

With sloth spotting, delicious food, and all logistics covered, this tour is perfect for couples, families, and nature lovers looking for a full-sensory experience in one of Costa Rica's most enchanting destinations.

Reasons to Book This Adventure

  • Hike to Río Celeste & Teñideros

    Explore Tenorio Volcano National Park and discover the famous Río Celeste Waterfall and the Teñideros, where two crystal-clear rivers merge and create the river's unique turquoise color.

  • Swim in Crystal-Blue Waters

    After the hike, enjoy a refreshing swim in the vibrant blue waters of Río Celeste at a nearby authorized swimming area.

  • Visit the Tree of Peace

    Discover the legendary Árbol de la Paz, a majestic ancient tree that has become a symbol of harmony and connection with nature.

  • Sloth Watching Experience

    Finish your adventure with a guided wildlife walk where you'll have the opportunity to spot sloths and other tropical species in their natural habitat.
