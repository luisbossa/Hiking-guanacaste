Why Choose This Tour?

Enjoy one of Guanacaste's most complete adventure experiences. Combine adrenaline, nature, wildlife, volcanic landscapes, horseback riding, hot springs and cultural experiences in a single unforgettable day.

Reasons to Book This Adventure

  • Exciting Canyon Adventure

    Explore the spectacular canyon landscapes and discover unique geological formations shaped by volcanic activity over thousands of years.

  • Horseback Riding Through Nature

    Ride scenic trails surrounded by tropical forests, rivers and beautiful Guanacaste landscapes.

  • Relax in Volcanic Hot Springs

    Unwind in naturally heated mineral-rich waters while enjoying incredible views of the surrounding rainforest and mountains.

  • Traditional Volcanic Mud Bath

    Cover your skin with volcanic mud known for its natural minerals and relaxing properties before rinsing off in thermal waters.
