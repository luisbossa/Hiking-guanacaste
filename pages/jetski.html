Why Choose This Tour?

Experience the thrill of riding a jet ski through the crystal-clear waters of Guanacaste's Pacific coastline. Discover hidden beaches, dramatic rock formations and incredible ocean scenery while enjoying one of the most exciting water adventures in Costa Rica.

Reasons to Book This Adventure

  • Explore Guanacaste's Coastline

    Cruise across the Pacific Ocean and discover some of the most beautiful beaches and hidden coves in Costa Rica.

  • Feel the Adrenaline

    Experience the excitement of riding a powerful jet ski across open waters while enjoying incredible coastal views.

  • Wildlife Encounters

    Depending on the season, you may spot dolphins, sea turtles, rays and tropical marine life along the route.

  • Perfect Ocean Adventure

    Ideal for couples, families and adventure seekers looking for a unique experience on Costa Rica's Pacific coast.
