About Hiking Guanacaste

Passionate local guides creating authentic nature, volcano, wildlife, and adventure experiences throughout Guanacaste and Costa Rica.

Who We Are

Hiking Guanacaste is a locally owned tour company dedicated to providing unforgettable outdoor adventures in some of Costa Rica's most spectacular natural destinations.

Our team consists of passionate tourism professionals, certified guides, and nature enthusiasts who love sharing the beauty, culture, wildlife, and hidden treasures of Guanacaste with visitors from around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect travelers with the incredible landscapes of Costa Rica through safe, educational, and memorable experiences while promoting sustainable tourism and environmental conservation.

We believe every tour should inspire a deeper appreciation for nature and provide meaningful memories that last a lifetime.

Why Choose Hiking Guanacaste?

  • Local experts with extensive knowledge of the region.
  • Personalized and friendly customer service.
  • Small group and private tour options.
  • Commitment to safety and sustainability.
  • Access to unique and authentic experiences.
  • Professional bilingual guides.

Our Commitment to Sustainability

Costa Rica is recognized worldwide for its commitment to environmental protection, and we proudly support that vision.

We encourage responsible tourism practices that help protect wildlife, preserve natural habitats, and support local communities throughout Guanacaste.
