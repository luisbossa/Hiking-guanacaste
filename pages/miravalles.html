Why Choose This Tour?

Discover one of Guanacaste's hidden natural treasures. Explore lush rainforest trails, spectacular waterfalls, volcanic landscapes and abundant wildlife while enjoying an unforgettable adventure near Miravalles Volcano.

Reasons to Book This Adventure

  • Explore Lush Rainforest Trails

    Walk beneath towering tropical trees and discover one of Costa Rica's richest ecosystems filled with colorful flora and incredible biodiversity.

  • Visit Hidden Waterfalls

    Enjoy scenic hikes leading to beautiful waterfalls surrounded by untouched rainforest and crystal-clear streams.

  • Observe Amazing Wildlife

    Spot monkeys, toucans, butterflies, iguanas and many tropical bird species that call the Miravalles region home.

  • Experience Volcano Landscapes

    Enjoy breathtaking views of Miravalles Volcano and learn about the region's unique geological and ecological importance.
