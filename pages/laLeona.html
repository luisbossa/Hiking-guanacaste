Why Choose This Tour?

This experience combines three of the best natural adventures in Guanacaste into one unforgettable day. Perfect for couples, families, and solo travelers looking to enjoy waterfalls, rivers, caves, and volcanic hot springs in a single tour.

Reasons to Book This Adventure

  • Lazy River Tubing at Poza Los Coyotes

    Start your day floating gently down a clear turquoise river in Curubande, Liberia. Enjoy a relaxing tubing experience surrounded by canyon walls and tropical scenery. You'll also take a short hike to admire a beautiful cascade. Plus, there's an optional cliff jump for the adventurous!

  • Hike to the Hidden La Leona Waterfall

    This 1.5-hour guided hike takes you through the lush, green forest of Costa Rica. You'll spot wildlife, walk along scenic trails, and eventually reach a stunning secret: La Leona Waterfall. Hidden inside a cave where you can swim and take breathtaking photos.

  • Natural Hot Springs & Volcanic Mud Bath

    Relax in natural hot springs at Canon de la Vieja Lodge and try a rejuvenating volcanic mud bath, known for its skin-cleansing benefits.
