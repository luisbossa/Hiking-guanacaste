Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our tours, bookings, transportation, and adventure experiences in Costa Rica.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes. We highly recommend booking in advance to guarantee availability, especially during peak travel seasons and holidays.

How can I make a reservation?

Reservations can be made directly through our website, WhatsApp, email, or social media channels.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, bank transfers, and other secure payment methods available during checkout.

Do your tours include transportation?

Most of our tours include round-trip transportation from selected locations in Guanacaste. Please check the specific tour details for confirmation.

What should I bring on a hiking tour?

  • Comfortable hiking shoes
  • Lightweight clothing
  • Sunscreen
  • Insect repellent
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Camera or smartphone
  • Rain jacket during the rainy season

Are your tours suitable for children?

Many of our tours are family-friendly. However, some adventure activities may have minimum age requirements for safety reasons.

What happens if it rains?

Costa Rica's tropical climate often includes rain. Most tours operate in light rain conditions. If severe weather affects safety, we may reschedule or offer alternative options.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes. Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled tour may qualify for a full refund, subject to our cancellation policy.

Are meals included?

Some tours include lunch, snacks, or beverages. Please review the "What's Included" section on each tour page.

Do I need previous hiking experience?

Not necessarily. We offer tours for all experience levels, from beginners to experienced hikers.

Are your guides certified?

Yes. Our guides are experienced local professionals with extensive knowledge of Costa Rica's wildlife, ecosystems, trails, and safety procedures.

Can I book a private tour?

Absolutely. We offer private and customized experiences for couples, families, groups, and corporate events.

What should I wear?

  • Breathable outdoor clothing
  • Comfortable hiking shoes or trail shoes
  • Hat or cap
  • Sunglasses
  • Light rain jacket if needed

Is Costa Rica safe for outdoor activities?

Costa Rica is one of the safest destinations in Central America and is world-renowned for ecotourism and adventure travel. We always follow strict safety standards during our tours.
